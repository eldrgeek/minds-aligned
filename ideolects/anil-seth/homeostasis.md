Homeostasis is the maintenance of physiological variables within viable bounds—the classical set-point style regulation of the internal milieu. In Seth's framework it is not merely background biology: interoceptive inference and autonomic reflexes are organized so that descending predictions enslave bodily states toward homeostatic (and, prospectively, allostatic) ends, tying perception and feeling to keeping the organism alive.

<strong>Anil Seth's lens:</strong> Seth (with Friston) relocates homeostasis inside predictive processing: bodily states are regulated by active inference, with precision-weighted prediction errors driving both perceptual updating and autonomic action, continuous with cybernetic ideas of predictive control. The term rules out treating consciousness as floating free of metabolic need and rules out generative models whose only job is accurate world-modelling. Where textbook homeostasis is a physiological engineering concept and allostasis stresses anticipatory change of set points, Seth's usage makes homeostatic viability the deep objective that shapes embodied selfhood and emotion—the beast machine's reason for inferring at all—so that feelings become control-oriented inferences about bodily condition rather than decorative afterthoughts on cognition.
