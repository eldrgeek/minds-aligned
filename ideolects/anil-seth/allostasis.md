Allostasis is anticipatory physiological regulation: the organism maintains viability not only by correcting errors after they occur (classical homeostasis) but by changing priors and setpoints so that bodily variables are predicted and controlled in advance of demand. In the hierarchical Bayesian framework Seth co-develops, allostasis appears as updates to the predictions that drive homeostatic reflex arcs, so interoceptive inference and bodily control share a single computational vocabulary of priors, prediction errors, and precision.

<strong>Anil Seth's lens:</strong> Allostasis is how the “beast machine” stays alive in a changing world and how conscious selfhood stays tied to control rather than to a static inner picture. Seth-linked work treats chronic failure of allostatic control (dyshomeostasis) and beliefs about that failure (allostatic self-efficacy) as pathways into fatigue and depression, making clinical phenomenology continuous with interoceptive inference. That rules out equating bodily regulation with only feedback homeostasis, and it departs from purely cognitive readings of predictive processing by making the point of inference the maintenance of physiological integrity. In his framework, allostasis is not a side topic in stress physiology; it is a core reason brains predict at all.
