Integrated information is a family of formal quantities that score how much a system’s dynamics generate information that is irreducible to the sum of independent parts—capturing joint differentiation (many distinct states) and integration (those states acting as a whole). In consciousness science it is the core mathematical ingredient of integrated information theory (IIT) and of related empirical proxies applied to neural time series.

<strong>Anil Seth's lens:</strong> Seth’s group endorses the *weak* program: use integrated-information ideas as scientifically productive measures and hypotheses about conscious level and dynamics, without accepting the strongest IIT claims that a particular formula fully identifies consciousness or that maximally irreducible cause–effect structures *are* experience. “The strength of weak IIT” is exactly that stance—keep the useful mathematics and the integration–differentiation insight; drop or suspend the metaphysical overreach. This rules out both dismissing integration measures outright and treating Φ as settled identity theory. Relative to orthodox Tononi-style IIT, Sethian integrated information is a tool among other complexity measures (causal density, signal diversity), always answerable to empirical dissociations across sleep, anaesthesia, and altered states rather than to axiomatic completeness alone.
