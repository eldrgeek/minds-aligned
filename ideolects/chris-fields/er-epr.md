ER = EPR is the conjecture that Einstein–Rosen bridges (spacetime wormholes) and Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen entanglement are two descriptions of the same underlying connection. In Fields's operational reading, two agents in a local-operations-and-classical-communication (LOCC) protocol cannot distinguish monogamous entanglement between their systems from a topological identification of points in their respective local spacetimes.

<strong>Chris Fields's lens:</strong> Fields and collaborators promote ER = EPR from a gravity-inspired conjecture to an operational theorem inside LOCC: if Alice and Bob can only act locally and exchange classical messages, entanglement and ER-type identification are experimentally equivalent for them. The construction does not depend on an embedding bulk geometry, so local spacetime topology becomes observer-relative, and non-traversability of ER bridges follows immediately from the same operational limits. This rules out treating wormholes as extra geometric machinery that observers could certify independently of entanglement structure, and it departs from purely geometric ER = EPR approaches that start from bulk duals. For Fields the result sits with holographic screens and quantum reference frames: classical spacetime relations available to finite agents are fixed by informational protocols, not by a God's-eye manifold.
