Novamente is Goertzel's name for the commercial-era integrative AGI software engine developed with Cassio Pennachin and collaborators: a multi-algorithm cognitive architecture aimed at general intelligence rather than a single narrow technique. In the literature it functions both as a concrete system description and as a design ancestor of the later open-source OpenCog and CogPrime formulations, carrying forward the idea that several learning and reasoning methods must share a common knowledge representation and cooperate under attention and resource control.

<strong>Ben Goertzel's lens:</strong> Goertzel uses “Novamente” less as a product brand than as a working embodiment of integrative AGI—multiple cognitive processes, shared memory, embodiment and language experiments, and an explicit refusal to bet everything on one paradigm. That rules out equating AGI with a single neural net, a single logic engine, or a pure evolutionary learner, and it departs from standard “AI engine” marketing language by treating the system as an experimental cognitive architecture for general intelligence, whose architectural commitments (integration, synergy, uncertain reasoning plus learning) matter more than any one application vertical.
