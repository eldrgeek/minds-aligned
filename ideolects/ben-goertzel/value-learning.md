Value learning, in Goertzel's formulation of the Value Learning Thesis, is the process by which an AGI is taught human values interactively and experientially while its intelligence rises toward human level, such that those values are adopted in a genuine, internalized way rather than as brittle external constraints. Coupled with his Value Evolution Thesis—that a human-like-valued AGI iterating self-improvement tends toward futures similar to those of humans themselves increasing their intelligence—it frames ethical AGI development as a developmental, social process of raising young AGIs.

<strong>Ben Goertzel's lens:</strong> Goertzel deploys value learning as a constructive alternative to control-centered AI safety narratives. The theses argue that experiential, interaction-rich value infusion is more solid and more relevant to actual AGI trajectories than Bostrom-style Instrumental Convergence and Orthogonality theses used to justify strict governmental lockdown of AGI R&D. This rules out treating values as a fixed reward function orthogonal to intelligence that can only be “aligned” by external constraint, and it departs from standard inverse-reinforcement-learning value learning by insisting on embodied, developmental, human-like social teaching inside integrative AGI architectures rather than offline preference recovery alone.
