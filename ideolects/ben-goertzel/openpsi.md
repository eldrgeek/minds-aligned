OpenPsi is Goertzel’s realization of Dietrich Dörner’s Psi cognitive model inside OpenCog: a computational layer for drives, urges, goals, and affective modulation that ties an integrative AGI architecture’s cognitive processes to motivational dynamics rather than treating goals as static objective functions handed to an optimizer. In this usage, Psi-style constructs supply the intentional and emotional scaffolding—what the system cares about, when it acts, and how internal states bias perception, planning, and learning—within the same hypergraph-and-process ecosystem as logic, procedure learning, and attention.

<strong>Ben Goertzel's lens:</strong> Goertzel does not import Psi as a standalone psychology simulation; OpenPsi is the motivational interface that makes integrative AGI *goal-directed under scarcity*, linking affect and urges to Atomspace-mediated cognition. That rules out pure reward-maximization RL as an adequate intentional architecture for human-like AGI, and it departs from standard “emotion AI” or app-level sentiment modules by embedding Psi dynamics in the full cognitive stack so that motivation can steer attention, inference, and procedure learning rather than only decorate behavior after decisions are made.
