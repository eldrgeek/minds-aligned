CogPrime is Goertzel’s comprehensive architecture for integrative, embodied artificial general intelligence: a detailed blueprint specifying how diverse algorithms and knowledge representations—sharing an Atomspace-style substrate and cooperating under cognitive synergy—should jointly produce human-level and eventually superhuman capability after sufficient experience in a rich enough world. It is the design-level counterpart to OpenCog implementations, covering core structures, learning and inference processes, and the emergent dynamics expected once the system is fully wired and educated rather than only partially implemented.

<strong>Ben Goertzel's lens:</strong> Goertzel treats CogPrime as an engineering theory of mind for AGI, not as a loose collection of AI techniques or a single algorithm claiming to be “the” intelligence. The architecture rules out both pure end-to-end deep learning and pure symbolic theorem-proving as complete AGI designs, insisting instead on multi-process integration plus embodiment and developmental experience. That departs from standard “cognitive architecture” usage that often means fixed production-system modules: CogPrime’s point is open-ended learning, self-modification potential, and synergy among processes that rewrite a shared hypergraph mind rather than pipe fixed outputs through a static box diagram.
