OpenCog Hyperon is Goertzel's next-generation AGI software framework: a largely from-the-ground-up rewrite and redesign of the earlier OpenCog system that preserves the same cognitive-synergy and integrative-intelligence principles while introducing new mathematical foundations, knowledge-representation structures, process models, and AI algorithms aimed at scaling toward human-level AGI and beyond. It is presented not as a single algorithm but as a practical path—core metagraph memory, rewrite-based cognition, and interoperable learning and reasoning components—for building beneficial general intelligence.

<strong>Ben Goertzel's lens:</strong> Hyperon is Goertzel's answer to the engineering gap between AGI theory and deployable systems: same patternist and synergetic philosophy as classical OpenCog/CogPrime, but with a redesigned substrate (including reflective metagraph rewriting and a more uniform "language of thought") intended to make deep integration of logic, learning, and attention tractable at scale. It rules out treating AGI as merely scaling narrow deep-learning models, and it departs from generic "cognitive architecture" catalogs by tying the software stack explicitly to a multi-decade theory of general intelligence and to a claim that open, integrative, self-modifying systems—not monolithic end-to-end nets—are the realistic route to beneficial AGI and ASI.
