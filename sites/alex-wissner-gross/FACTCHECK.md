the site was checked against a record in this archive's own `papers/` corpus or against his

## Corpus integrity

**zero** records outside the main component — the hyphenated surname is distinctive and the
harvest is clean. **No exclusions were necessary.** Two records appear twice (the Cu²⁺

version-of-record preference resolves both.

picture of a person whose main current output is not papers.

## Corrections made during this pass

1. **A claim was attributed to the wrong paper.** A dictionary entry for "rule ensemble"
   cited "Structure and Complexity in Rule Ensemble Cellular Automata" for the "first known
   analysis" claim. That record is an APS bulletin abstract with no text in this corpus; the
   claim belongs to "Pattern Formation without Favored Local Interactions." Citation moved
   and the sentence rewritten to quote the phrase.
2. **The roster affiliation was stale.** The hub listed him as "Harvard / MIT / Entropica."
   His own site now gives Founder and Chief Scientist of Physical Superintelligence. Updated
   on the hub as part of this pass.

## Deliberately not claimed

- **Self-reported tallies are omitted.** His site states counts of distinctions, patents,
  publications and talk views. None is checkable from this corpus, so none appears on the
  site — and the About panel says why.
- **No position taken on the Causal Entropic Forces argument.** It is a strong claim that
  has been contested in the literature. The site says so plainly, links the paper in the
  venue that published it, and does not adjudicate.
- **No implication of recent research activity.** The indexed record ends around 2021; one
  of the readings names that gap explicitly and tells the host to decline rather than
  reconstruct on anything later.
- **No claim about his AGI-26 session.**
