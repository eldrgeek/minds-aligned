This is the smallest corpus in the constellation — twenty-one indexed records — and the gap between it and its subject's public profile is the largest. The published record ends, on this evidence, around 2021; the person it belongs to is currently Founder and Chief Scientist of Physical Superintelligence, writes a widely read newsletter, co-hosts a podcast, and has advised many companies. None of that produces a DOI, so none of it is here. The archive reads this as a limit worth naming rather than papering over: a citation database is a good instrument for academics and a poor one for people whose main output is not papers. Two consequences follow for anyone using this archive. First, an absence of recent records is not evidence of an absence of recent work. Second, the AI host of this archive can only answer from what is indexed — so on anything after 2021, or anything about ventures, talks or writing, it should say it does not have the material rather than reconstruct it.
