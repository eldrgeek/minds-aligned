## 3. The Max-Neef correspondence — state plainly, don't debunk

conxiOS's "nine universal needs" architecture corresponds, need-for-need, to economist
Manfred Max-Neef's nine fundamental human needs from *Human Scale Development* (1991):
Subsistence, Protection, Affection, Understanding, Participation, Idleness, Creation,
Identity, Freedom — a framework taught in development economics for over three decades.
conxiOS's site says the architecture was "discovered," not invented, in "ancient
philosophy, neuroscience, depth psychology, and systems theory," and does not name
Max-Neef. This is presented on the site as a fact worth noting for how a reader weighs
provenance, respectfully and without accusation — the vocabulary may have reached Diaz
indirectly, through downstream popularizations of Max-Neef's work that don't themselves
cite him. Do not escalate this into a claim of plagiarism; the corpus does not support
that and it was not asked for.
