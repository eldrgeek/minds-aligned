Read the record in order and one word keeps recurring under different jobs: alignment. In the advertising years — Cossette through Leo Burnett to Young & Rubicam Toronto — the job was aligning a brand's voice to a client's business. At Sunday+Night, founded after that arc, the unit of alignment shrinks to the person: "Being + Business Alignment" workshops that culminate in what the agency calls a client's own "Magnum Opus," on the claim that "energy is sacred. It is the currency of creation." conxiOS extends the same move one step further, from person to machine: "You can't align AI with humans when humans aren't aligned with themselves." Three careers, one verb, applied at three different scales — brand, person, and now, as he frames it, artificial intelligence itself.
