β-VAE is a small modification with a large downstream footprint in this corpus: a single coefficient weighting the latent bottleneck against reconstruction. What follows from it in this archive is a research programme rather than a result. "Understanding disentangling in β-VAE" (2018) supplies a rate-distortion account of *why* the aligned representations emerge. "DARLA" (2017) turns the representation into a capability — an agent that "learns to see before learning to act" and transfers zero-shot. "SCAN" (2017) makes the concepts hierarchical and recombinable. And "Towards a Definition of Disentangled Representations" (2018) does the unusual thing: it notes there is "no generally agreed-upon definition of disentangling, not least because it is unclear how to formalise the notion", and proposes one. The archive reads this sequence as a lab formalising the concept its own results depend on, after the results — which is worth noticing, because the more common order in machine learning is to keep using an undefined word for as long as it keeps working.
